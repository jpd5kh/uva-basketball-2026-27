Players:

- Adam Oumiddoch - primarily playing out of corners; smooth jumper, able to get to middle of the floor for first bucket

- Luigi Suigo - insane size, elite rim protector; will affect shots just by presence under the rim; very patient and cautious in what passes/DHOs he makes, not afraid to keep in a DHO and work through secondary reads/continued movement; great passer out of the post, making perfect reads of the help (very quick decision-maker); a little vulnerable vs. stretch bigs able to get downhill (getting beat when Gurdak faces up and can create)

- Darryl Simmons II - brings energy on first defensive possession; quick pest who will defend 94 feet, spark off the bench; extremely quick to his spots, loves to spot up in midrange; sometimes switches a little too easily defensively, see 9:22 2nd and 10:30 2nd
